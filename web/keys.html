Add your Alpaca keys

The last step. Paste the two keys from your Alpaca paper account and press Start Charles. Charles starts trading practice money, and this page opens your dashboard.

Where are my keys?
  1. Open app.alpaca.markets and log in. No account yet? Sign up (free).
  2. At the top left, check it says Paper. If it doesn't, click the account name there and pick your paper account.
  3. On the Home page, find API Keys on the right and press Generate New Keys.
  4. Copy the API Key (it starts with PK) and the Secret Key into the boxes below.

Your keys go straight to this server, which is yours. charles-bot.xyz never sees them. Charles checks them with Alpaca once, then keeps them in its keys file.
